# OMI videos

Two videos, two jobs (see the video playbook): a 30-second ad for X, and a 3–5 minute demo for judges
(IEEE ClimateChain asks for 3–5 minutes). The demo shows the real product running, no mockups, and ends on a
payout transaction anyone can open on the Arc explorer. Every number below comes from the live site.

Amina is an illustrative farmer, not a real person; the film says so on screen ("Amina is a composite").

---
